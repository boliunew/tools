08｜简历改写工作坊：前后对照

下面用一位虚构的堆场主管「Leo Chen」做示范，看一份普通简历怎么改成有说服力的版本。

改写前（常见问题）

LEO CHEN
Age 38, Married, 1234 Main St, Upland CA 91786
Objective: Looking for a good job with growth opportunity.

Yard Supervisor, ABC Logistics, 2021-now
- Responsible for yard
- Supervise drivers
- Check containers and trailers
- Other duties as assigned

问题：写了年龄、婚姻、详细地址；Objective 是空话；全是职责没有结果；没有关键词；没有日期月份。

改写后

LEO CHEN
Upland, CA | (909) 555-0123 | leo.chen.ops@gmail.com | linkedin.com/in/leochen-ops

SUMMARY
Yard and transportation supervisor with 5 years in high-volume
distribution. Cut average trailer dwell time 18% and kept a
12-person, 2-shift team at zero recordable injuries for 2 years.
Bilingual English/Mandarin, conversational Spanish.

EXPERIENCE
Yard Supervisor | ABC Logistics, Ontario, CA | Mar 2021 – Present
- Manage a 300+ trailer yard across day and night shifts,
  coordinating 40–60 daily inbound/outbound moves with dock teams
- Reduced average trailer dwell time 18% by re-sequencing dock
  appointments and adding a mid-shift yard check
- Lead 12 yard drivers and clerks; zero recordable injuries in
  24 months through daily safety huddles and PIT refreshers
- Built an Excel tracker for container and seal checks that cut
  mis-placed trailers from ~10 to ~2 per week
- Main contact for 15+ carriers on detention, damages and
  late pickups

SKILLS & CERTIFICATIONS
Yard Management System (YMS) | WMS | Excel (pivot tables, XLOOKUP)
OSHA 30 | Forklift / PIT Trainer | ISO 6346 container checks
English, Mandarin (native), Spanish (conversational)

EDUCATION
Bachelor's degree, Logistics Management (foreign degree, U.S. equivalent)

改了什么

改动为什么
删掉年龄、婚姻、详细地址美国招聘不该看这些，写了反而减分
Objective 改成 Summary说你能带来什么，不是你想要什么
每条都有动词 + 数字招聘人员几秒钟就看到结果
写出系统名、证书名让 ATS 和招聘人员搜得到
日期写到月份美国简历惯例
双语写进摘要物流现场很看重

自己动手

  1. 把你现在的简历每一条，用「问自己：因为我，什么变好了？」改写一遍；
  2. 每条至少找一个数字（人数、柜数、百分比、时间、次数）；
  3. 对照目标 JD 的前 10 个关键词，看覆盖了几个；
  4. 存成纯文本检查一遍格式，见 02 简历。

数字都要说得出来源，不能编。
