10｜面试 40 问：思路和追问

每题给出考察点和回答思路，用你自己的故事填。英文问法照抄，方便你听懂。

开场（1–6）

  1. Tell me about yourself. 60–90 秒：现在做什么 → 2 个最强成果 → 为什么申请这个职位。不讲出生地和家庭。
  2. Why are you looking for a new job? 说往前走（成长、更大的范围），不说老板坏话。
  3. Why do you want to work here? 提到你查过的具体事：业务、地点、扩张、文化。
  4. What do you know about our company? 两三句：做什么、客户是谁、最近新闻。
  5. Walk me through your resume. 按时间顺序，每段一句话加一个成果。
  6. Why should we hire you? 对准 JD 前三个要求，各用一个事实证明。

行为题（7–20）：用 STAR

  1. A time you handled a conflict between team members.
  2. A time you dealt with a difficult driver, carrier or customer.
  3. A time you made a mistake. 真实的小错 + 你怎么补救 + 学到什么。
  4. A time you improved a process. 一定要有前后数字。
  5. A time you handled an emergency or safety issue.
  6. A time you had to meet a tight deadline.
  7. A time you disagreed with your manager. 表现出尊重和用事实说话。
  8. A time you coached an underperforming employee.
  9. A time you had to enforce a rule people didn't like.
  10. A time you worked with limited staff.
  11. A time you learned a new system quickly.
  12. A time you went above and beyond.
  13. A time you had to make a decision without all the information.
  14. Your biggest accomplishment.

管理和运营（21–32）

  1. How do you plan staffing for a shift? 看预测量、历史数据、缺勤率，留机动人员。
  2. Which KPIs do you track? 举 3 个和岗位相关的，说你怎么用它们。
  3. How do you handle an employee who keeps breaking safety rules? 先当面纠正 → 记录 → 再培训 → 按公司纪律流程，安全不让步。
  4. How do you handle a no-call no-show? 按公司政策，记录，事后谈话，也想办法顶班。
  5. How do you motivate hourly workers? 公平排班、及时认可、讲清楚为什么。
  6. What would you do in your first 30 days? 学流程、认识人、看数据、找一个小改进。
  7. How do you prioritize when everything is urgent? 安全 > 客户承诺 > 成本，说具体例子。
  8. How do you communicate with other departments? 固定交接会、共享看板、及时升级问题。
  9. What systems have you used? 列系统名和你用它做什么。
  10. How do you handle peak season?
  11. Tell me about a time you reduced cost or waste.
  12. How do you train new hires?

收尾（33–40）

  1. What are your strengths? 两个，各配一个证据。
  2. What is your greatest weakness? 真实、可改进、正在改的。
  3. Where do you see yourself in 5 years? 在这个领域成长、承担更多责任。
  4. What are your salary expectations? 先问预算，再给区间，见 05 Offer。
  5. Are you able to work nights / weekends / overtime? 诚实回答，能接受的范围说清楚。
  6. When can you start? 通常是「两周通知之后」。
  7. Do you have any questions for us? 准备 3 个，见 04 面试。
  8. Is there anything else you'd like us to know? 用一句话再强调你最匹配的一点。

常见追问

面试官常会追问：*What exactly did you do? How did you measure that? What would you do differently?* 准备故事时，每个故事都先自问这三句。
