内推和招聘人员私信

1. 请认识的人内推

Hi [Name], hope you're doing well! I saw that [Company] is hiring an Operations Supervisor (Job ID 12345: [link]). I've been running a 300-trailer yard at [Current Company] for the past few years and I think it's a strong fit. Would you be open to referring me, or sharing a bit about the team? I've attached my resume to make it easy. Thanks so much!

2. 请不认识的员工（同职能）

Hi [Name], I'm a yard supervisor in the Inland Empire and I'm interested in the Operations Supervisor role at [Company]. I noticed you work on the operations team there. Would you have 10 minutes for a quick call so I can learn what the team values? No pressure at all — thank you!

3. 联系招聘人员

Hi [Name], I just applied for the Operations Supervisor role (Job ID 12345). I bring 5 years of high-volume yard and transportation experience, a team of 12 with zero recordable injuries for 2 years, and I'm bilingual in English and Mandarin. I'd love to be considered — happy to share more anytime.

4. 跟进（投递 7–10 天后）

Hi [Name], I wanted to follow up on my application for the Operations Supervisor role. I'm still very interested and would welcome the chance to talk. Thank you for your time!

5. 感谢帮忙的人

Thank you again for referring me — I really appreciate it. I'll keep you posted on how it goes!

要点：短（LinkedIn 消息 300 字符以内最好）、有职位编号和链接、说清楚为什么匹配、给对方方便。
