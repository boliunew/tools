谈薪话术

被问期望薪资（还没拿 Offer）

I'd like to learn more about the role first. Could you share the budgeted range for this position?

对方坚持：

Based on my research for similar roles in this area and my experience, I'm looking at a range of $64,000 to $70,000, depending on the full benefits package.

拿到 Offer 后提出更高数字

Thank you — I'm really excited about this opportunity. Based on the market data for this role and my track record of [key result], I was hoping for a base closer to $[target]. Is there any flexibility?

工资动不了，谈别的

I understand. Would it be possible to include a sign-on bonus of $[X], or revisit the salary at my six-month review?

Could the company cover my [certification] training?

要考虑时间

Thank you. Could I have until [day] to review the full offer and benefits?

接受

Thank you so much. I'm happy to accept the offer at $[X] with a start date of [date]. I look forward to receiving the updated offer letter.

婉拒

Thank you for the offer and for your time throughout the process. After careful thought, I've decided to accept another position. I hope our paths cross again.
