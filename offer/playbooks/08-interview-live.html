模拟面试实战

90 秒自我介绍（示例，虚构）

I'm a yard and transportation supervisor with five years in high-volume distribution here in the Inland Empire. Right now I run a 300-trailer yard across two shifts with a team of twelve. Two things I'm proud of: I cut average trailer dwell time by about 18 percent by re-sequencing dock appointments, and my team has gone two years without a recordable injury. I'm bilingual in English and Mandarin and can get by in Spanish on the floor. I'm looking for an operations supervisor role where I can take on a larger team and more of the planning side, which is why this position caught my attention.

结构：现在做什么 → 两个成果 → 加分点 → 为什么是这个职位。

一道行为题的完整回答

Q：Tell me about a time you dealt with a safety violation.

S: Last summer during peak, one of our yard drivers started skipping the walk-around check to save time. T: I had to stop it without hurting morale when everyone was already stretched. A: I pulled him aside privately that same shift, explained why the check matters and showed him a near-miss report from another site. I documented the conversation, and the next morning I ran a five-minute huddle for the whole crew on why we never skip checks, even in peak. I also adjusted the schedule so the check had a protected two minutes. R: We had no further skipped checks that season, and the driver later became one of our best trainers.

练习方法

  1. 找朋友或用 AI 扮演面试官，按 面试 40 问 抽 8 题；
  2. 用手机录视频；
  3. 回看时检查：每个答案多长（目标 1.5–2 分钟）、有没有数字、有没有「I」、有没有口头禅（um、like）；
  4. 只挑 2 个问题改，再练一次。

视频面试检查

  • 摄像头和眼睛平齐，光从前面来；
  • 背景简单，手机静音；
  • Zoom / Teams 名字改成英文全名；
  • 提前 5 分钟进入，测试声音。
